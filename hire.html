FAQ + Fine Print
  • What is the free initial meeting? It is a short 15-minute intro call for creative consulting, photography inquiries, and pet-sitting meet-and-greets so we can see if it is a good fit and confirm the basics before booking.
  • What should I send before the call? A short summary of your project, your ideal timeline, and any links, references, or questions you already have.
  • What happens after the call? If it feels like a fit, I will send the next steps, pricing, or booking details.
  • Do I have to book after the free meeting? No. It is simply a low-pressure intro to make sure we are on the same page.
  • Refunds and Policies: All sales are final unless otherwise discussed.
  • Booking Policy: A retainer is required to reserve your date for pet sitting, photography, or consulting and will go toward the final cost. If you cancel 7 or more days before your service, the retainer is returned. If you cancel between 7 days and 48 hours before the service, 50% of the retainer is returned. Cancellations within 48 hours are not refunded.
  • Custom Requests: Absolutely. Reach out and let us talk about your project.
