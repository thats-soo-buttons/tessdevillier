Booking Notes

Travel And Scheduling

Travel outside Baton Rouge is available. Travel fees may apply depending on distance and timeline. Rush turnaround is available by quote.

Deposit And Delivery

A 50% retainer secures your date. Final gallery is delivered after the remaining balance is paid.

Reschedule And Cancellation

Retainers are non-refundable but can be transferred one time if you reschedule at least 72 hours in advance. Weather reschedules are flexible and handled case by case.

Usage And Licensing

Portrait packages include personal-use rights. Commercial use, paid ads, or brand licensing are quoted separately. RAW files are not included.
