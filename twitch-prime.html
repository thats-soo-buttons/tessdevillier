🎮 Free Twitch Prime Sub

Support me for free — seriously, it costs you nothing.

If you have Amazon Prime, you have a free Twitch channel subscription you can give out every single month. It directly supports me at zero cost to you.

  1. Go to twitch.tv/thats_soo_buttons
  2. Click the Subscribe button (the purple button near the top of the page, below the stream).
  3. In the subscription dialog, look for the option "Subscribe with Prime" — it should appear if you're logged into Twitch and have Prime.
  4. If you haven't linked your Amazon account yet, Twitch will prompt you to. Just sign in with your Amazon credentials — it's quick and secure.
  5. Confirm. Done! 💜 You're subscribed and I get paid.
Heads up: Twitch Prime subs don't auto-renew. Your free sub resets at the start of each month, but you have to manually re-subscribe each time. Set a little monthly reminder and it keeps supporting me every month for free!
