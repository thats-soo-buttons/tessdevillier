## Trademark / Naming Notes
- Avoid implying official affiliation with any deck or publisher.
- Do not use protected logos, deck marks, or brand names without permission.
- If using a deck name, verify trademark status and permitted use.

## Files to Keep as Proof
Store these in a local licenses folder (not just bookmarks):
- License text files
- Screenshots of license pages and terms
- Receipts and purchase confirmations
- Creator permissions or contracts
- Source URLs with access date

## Suggested Attribution Section (In-Game)
Use this short form inside your game menu:

"Some artwork, icons, and audio used in this project are licensed from third-party creators. Full attributions and license details are available in the Licenses and Credits page."
