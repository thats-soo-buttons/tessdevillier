Music + Lyrics

My brain has enough worldbuilding ideas to fuel three novels and a spin-off, but not always enough hours to write them cover to cover. So when a scene, character, or weird little universe shows up, I turn it into a song or a writing prompt so the idea does not go to waste.

To hear more lyric concepts and story ideas, explore the whimsical side. If you are interested in taking on lyrics, please contact me at contact@tessdevillier.com.
