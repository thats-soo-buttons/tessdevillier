Pets FAQ

Do you require a meet & greet?

Yes. I like to meet each pet and learn their routine before the first booking.

What should I leave for my pet?

Food, leash, litter or waste supplies, medications, and anything that helps them stay comfortable.

Will you send updates?

Yes. I can send photo and text updates so you know how your pet is doing.

Do you give medications?

Yes, if your pet has a routine and clear instructions.

Can you stay overnight?

Yes. Overnight house and pet sitting is my preferred sit, and I recommend it for pets that are more nervous.

Why are your rates higher than average?

This is my full-time job, not side income. You’re paying for dedicated in-home care, more consistent attention, and the peace of mind that your pets and home are being looked after while you’re away.

How do payments work?

Cash, Zelle, Cash App, or Rover. A 50% deposit is due up front for direct bookings, and the remaining balance is due at completion. Rover pricing may be higher because of Rover’s fee.

What Is Included

  • Plant watering schedule and light home checks
  • General occupied-home presence and routine upkeep

Pricing Guide

  • House + plant sitting (no pets): starting at $60/night
  • Extended stay discount example: 7+ nights from $385/week
