Pets FAQ

Do you require a meet & greet?

Yes. I like to meet each pet and learn their routine before the first booking.

What should I leave for my pet?

Food, leash, litter or waste supplies, medications, and anything that helps them stay comfortable.

Will you send updates?

Yes. I can send photo and text updates so you know how your pet is doing.

Do you give medications?

Yes, if your pet has a routine and clear instructions.

Can you stay overnight?

Yes. Overnight house and pet sitting is my preferred sit, and I recommend it for pets that are more nervous.

How do payments work?

Cash, Zelle, Cash App, or Rover. Rover pricing may be higher because of Rover’s fee.
