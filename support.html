🎁 Support For Free (w/ Amazon Prime Twitch Sub)

Important: Prime sub is not auto-renewed. You must resubscribe manually each month.

1A. If You Don't Have Twitch Yet

  1. Go to Twitch and click Sign Up.
  2. Create account with email or phone.
  3. Verify account via confirmation prompt.

1B. Link Twitch to Amazon Prime

  1. Log in to Amazon Prime account.
  2. Go to Prime Gaming and sign in with Twitch account.
  3. Follow prompts to connect accounts.
  4. Confirm Prime benefits appear in Twitch subscription options.

1C. Use Your Free Monthly Sub

  1. Open Twitch channel page.
  2. Click Subscribe.
  3. Choose free Prime subscription option.
  4. Complete confirmation.

1D. Come Back Every Month

  • Prime sub expires monthly.
  • It does not auto-renew like paid recurring subs.
  • Resubscribe monthly to keep support active at no extra cost.
