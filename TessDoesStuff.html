About Me

I am based in Baton Rouge. I help people shape stories, visuals, and creative direction in ways that are practical and emotionally clear. I am independent, not a corporate agency, and I care a lot about work that feels human.

Services include pet care, photography, worldbuilding, and creative consulting.
