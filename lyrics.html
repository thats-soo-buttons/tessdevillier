A note to musicians, singers, and producers: If you would like to use any of these lyrics, contact me and we can work out an arrangement that is fair for everyone.

Start Here: Simple Lyric Use Terms

If you want to use a lyric, email me first so we can agree on terms. I am open to beginner-friendly collaborations.

  • Credit required: "Lyrics by Tess Devillier"
  • No release until we both agree in writing
  • We will decide together: one-time fee, split, or license
  • I am open to indie artists, singers, producers, and bands

When you contact me, include: song title, style/genre, where you plan to release it, and your timeline.
