XYTHIANS: An Omni-Media Experience

XYZTHIANS is a universe built across books, music, puzzles, TikTok, physical coins, and hidden easter eggs. Each artifact is a unique media experience—some contain secret puzzles, some are interactive, and all connect across platforms. From audio tracks and narrated stories to real-world scavenger hunts and AR, the project invites discovery and participation.

Vision & Impact

My goal is to foster creativity, community, and curiosity. XYZTHIANS is designed to engage audiences through interactive storytelling, cross-site easter eggs, TikTok puzzles, and physical artifacts. The project encourages exploration, collaboration, and discovery—making every participant part of the lore. Through interactive content, call and response, and audience submissions, participants become part of the mythos and help empower it—making the universe feel believable and real.

Gods' Calling Coin Sweepstakes

Each god has only one calling coin to give out as a favor. When a new XYZTHIANS book is released, a coin from the gods of that sector will be released into the world with a golden ticket aspect—a sweepstakes for those who find it. The finder will receive a special favor or prize, making the mythos interactive and rewarding.
