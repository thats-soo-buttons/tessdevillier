Free Support Guide

You can support me without paying extra out of pocket by using your included monthly Twitch sub from Amazon Prime. I still get paid, and you do not get an extra charge.

Quick truth: Prime sub is not auto-renewed. You have to come back each month and re-subscribe manually.

1. If You Do Not Have Twitch Yet

  1. Go to Twitch and click Sign Up.
  2. Create your account with email or phone.
  3. Verify your account when Twitch sends confirmation.

2. Link Twitch to Amazon Prime

  1. Log in to your Amazon Prime account.
  2. Go to Prime Gaming and sign in with your Twitch account.
  3. Follow prompts to connect the two accounts.
  4. Confirm Prime benefits show up in Twitch subscription options.

3. Use Your Free Monthly Sub

  1. Open my Twitch page.
  2. Click the Subscribe button.
  3. Choose the free Prime subscription option.
  4. Complete confirmation.

4. Come Back Every Month

  • Your Prime sub expires each month.
  • It does not auto-renew like paid recurring subs.
  • Re-sub once a month to keep support active at no extra cost.
